cua-s1.js form filling decisions in the browser

cua-s1-forms reads a form field and a document and decides: fill it with one of the document's values, tick it, click it, or leave it alone. 706k parameters, 3 MB, one forward pass for the whole form, on your CPU. Nothing is typed until you press Apply.
